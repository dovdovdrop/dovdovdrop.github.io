Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026 · Applies to the Android app DovdovDrop (package com.dovdov.dovdovdrop)

DovdovDrop sends contacts and files straight from one phone to another over NFC, Bluetooth / Wi-Fi (Google Nearby Connections) and QR codes. It has no accounts and no servers of its own, so your information stays on your phone and goes only to the device you choose.

In short

  • No ads. DovdovDrop shows no advertising and contains no advertising SDK.
  • No tracking or analytics. No analytics, crash-reporting or profiling libraries.
  • No account, no server. We do not run a backend. We cannot see, store or receive your data.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with advertisers, data brokers or anyone else.
  • You are in control. Every permission is optional to revoke, and you can clear your history at any time.

3. What leaves your device

Data leaves your phone only when you start a share, and only to the device you share with:

  • Tap (NFC): your contact card is passed to the phone you tap over a very short-range radio link.
  • QR code: your card, or a connection code, is encoded into a QR code shown on your screen. Anyone who scans the code can read what it contains, so show it only to people you want to share with.
  • Nearby: files and contact cards go directly to the other phone over Bluetooth / Wi-Fi using Google's Nearby Connections. While sharing, the app advertises a display name (the device name you set in Settings, otherwise your profile name, otherwise "DovdovDrop") so the other person can recognise you. That name may be visible to other nearby devices running the app.

None of these transfers pass through a server operated by us.

Whatever you send is received by the other person and is then under their control. Only share with people you trust.

5. Third-party components

  • Google Play Services (Nearby Connections). Provides the device-to-device link for Nearby sharing. It runs on your phone and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Google Play Services may handle technical data on your device under its own terms. DovdovDrop does not send Google any of your contacts or files.
  • Google ML Kit (barcode scanning). Reads QR codes from the camera preview on your device.
  • Open-source libraries for the interface, local database, camera and QR generation. They run inside the app and do not send data anywhere.

The app declares the Android INTERNET permission because Google's Nearby Connections library may use local networks to connect two phones. DovdovDrop itself does not send your data to any internet server.

7. Storage and Android backup

Your contact card, share history and settings are kept in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read this storage.

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include this app data in your Google account backup (and in phone-to-phone transfers) under Google's terms. We cannot access that backup. You can turn it off in Android Settings → Google → Backup.

Files you receive are saved to your public Downloads folder, so other apps you have allowed to access files may be able to see them.

8. Your choices and controls

  • Edit or clear your card: My Card → Edit.
  • Delete history: Settings → Clear share history, or remove single items from History.
  • Revoke permissions: Android Settings → Apps → DovdovDrop → Permissions.
  • Delete everything: clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app.
  • Received files: delete them from your Downloads folder like any other file.

9. Retention and deletion

Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Data on your phone stays until you delete it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Data you have already sent to another person's device is under that person's control and cannot be removed by us.

10. Security

Your data is stored in the app's private storage and is not uploaded anywhere by the app. Device-to-device links use the protections built into NFC and Google's Nearby Connections, and Android's permission system limits what the app can access. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so keep your phone's software up to date and use a screen lock.

11. Children

DovdovDrop is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. The app has no accounts and sends nothing to us, so no information about children is collected or held by us.
